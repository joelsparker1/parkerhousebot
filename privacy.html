Parker House Bot: privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026.

Parker House Bot is a private assistant used only by the members of one household. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

Only with the account holder's consent through Google's sign-in, and only for that person's own account:

  • Gmail, read-only, to find mail that needs attention;
  • Gmail drafts, to prepare replies for the account holder to review. It never sends email;
  • Google Calendar, read-only, to list the day's events.

How the data is used and kept

  • It is used only to produce the household's own briefs and reminders.
  • The app runs on a computer in the household's home. Its sign-in credentials stay there.
  • To write a brief, relevant text is processed by Anthropic's Claude API under Anthropic's commercial terms; it is not used to train models.
  • Data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any other purpose.

Google user data

Parker House Bot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Anyone who signed in can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
